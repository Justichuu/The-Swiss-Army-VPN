# Colour key

Green and red stay opposite. Left is go. Right is stop. Grey means we have not proven either.

## Eye

| Colour | Hex | Lid | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| green | `#2CC478` | shut | Hidden. Traffic is not observable. |
| red | `#E24448` | open | Watched. Traffic can be seen, or the lock is hurting you. |
| grey | `#808694` | half | Unknown. |

The mandala tints to the same colour. One object. Not two icons fighting.

The window is 418 wide. The two big buttons are the same size. The four small ones are the same size. The three monitor checks sit on one row. That is the symmetry. Do not break it for a new feature. Add a row, or make a pair.

# Art

The joy is already in the object. Do not glue extra cuteness on it.

## The eye

When you are hidden, it shuts. When you are watched, it looks at you. That is not a mascot. That is the truth of the state, drawn so a person who does not read status text can still feel it.

The mandala is the same colour as the eye. One piece.

## The honest line

Some of the small button hints are dry. Keep them if they stay true. Do not add jokes that hide a risk.

## What not to add

- Confetti on connect
- A score
- A streak
- A character that begs you to upgrade

If you want to give something back, add a language, a caption, or a witness. That is love that lands.
